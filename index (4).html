02 • Carbon Tax

What is Carbon Tax?

Understanding the global direction of climate policy — and why it matters for Bintoo Green.

What is it?

A carbon tax is a fee charged on fuels, industries, or activities based on the amount of carbon dioxide they emit. The more pollution produced, the more tax is paid.

Why is it needed?

  • Reduce greenhouse gas emissions
  • Encourage clean energy and innovation
  • Make polluters financially responsible
  • Support climate action and sustainability

When did it start?

The modern carbon tax began in 1990, when Finland became the first country to introduce a national carbon tax. Since then, many countries have adopted carbon pricing systems.

What are the benefits?

  • Helps lower emissions
  • Creates government revenue for climate programs
  • Accelerates green technology adoption
  • Supports long-term environmental responsibility

“The more pollution produced, the more tax is paid.”

There is no single worldwide carbon tax rule — countries use carbon taxes, emissions trading systems (ETS), fuel-based charges, carbon credits and carbon border mechanisms.

04 • 1990 → 2050

The Global Carbon Tax Journey

A timeline towards a sustainable & low-carbon global economy.

The Beginning 1990

Finland Carbon Tax

Finland becomes the world's first country to introduce a carbon tax on fossil fuels — and still operates it today.

Cap & Trade 2005

EU ETS

The European Union launches the Emissions Trading System — one of the world's major mandatory carbon markets.

Global Expansion 2010–2020

Global Carbon Markets

Carbon markets expand globally — Europe, Canada, Asia and the Americas adopt carbon-tax and pricing models.

Asia Rises 2021–2025

China + UK + India

China ETS goes live, UK ETS strengthens, and India expands CCTS (Carbon Credit Trading Scheme).

Border Accountability 2026

EU CBAM

The EU Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism enters its definitive phase from 1 January 2026.

The Green Decade 2030

More Carbon Pricing

Widespread adoption of carbon pricing and stricter green-industry requirements across the globe.

The Destination 2050

Net-Zero Global Economy

A net-zero, resilient and low-carbon global economy becomes a reality.
